Sultan Omar 'Ali Saifuddien Mosque

Located in the heart of Bandar Seri Begawan, this famous mosque is recognised for its golden dome, marble architecture and peaceful lagoon.

Kampong Ayer

Known as the Water Village, Kampong Ayer is a historic settlement featuring traditional houses built on stilts above the Brunei River.

Ulu Temburong National Park

Explore untouched rainforest, nature trails and canopy walkways in one of Brunei's most remarkable natural destinations.

Jerudong Park

Enjoy a fun-filled visit to this amusement park, with entertainment and attractions for families and visitors.

Brunei Museum

Located in Kota Batu, the museum is an important heritage site dedicated to Brunei's history and culture. It is temporarily closed for refurbishment.
