🚖

Getting Around

Download ride-hailing apps like DART for easy travel between cafes, night markets, and Masjids. Public buses and water taxis across the Brunei River are also readily available.

💳

Visa & Currency

Brunei uses the Brunei Dollar (BND), which is pegged 1:1 with the Singapore Dollar (SGD). Cash is recommended for Pasar Malams and youth flea markets.

☕

Peaceful Routine

Plan your mornings early for cool runs or coffee at local cafes. Keep Friday afternoons (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM) free as all local shops close for prayers.
